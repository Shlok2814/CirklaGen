# Cirkla Tray LoRA Dataset & Curation Plan (Phase 2)

> **Specification Note**: This plan outlines the exact methodology for constructing a 20–40 image dataset from the 9 raw tray photographs and evaluating the fine-tuned LoRA. No models have been trained yet; this document specifies the protocol for future execution.

---

## 1. Expanding 9 Photos into 20–40 Training Samples

The raw asset pool consists of 9 high-resolution photographic captures of Cirkla #5 molded-fiber beef packages (`assets/raw_trays/`). To train an effective Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) without overfitting to specific backgrounds or studio tables, we generate a curated dataset of **28–36 augmented training pairs** (image + `.txt` caption).

### 1.1 Augmentation Strategies

1. **Multi-Scale Perspective Crops**:
   - **Full Package Establishing Shots (6–8 images)**: Entire tray visible with outer flange margins intact, showing rectangular footprint with rounded corners and uniform rim width.
   - **Fluting & Sidewall Crops (8–10 images)**: Close crops focusing on vertical scalloped ribs, wall draft angles (1.7-inch depth), and molded corner transitions.
   - **Corner Pyramid Structural Crops (6–8 images)**: Macro details isolating the molded corner pyramid reinforcement geometry that gives the tray stacking rigidity.
   - **Base Panel & Branding Crops (4–6 images)**: Underside views showing the recessed rectangular floor and debossed CIRKLA lettering.

2. **Re-Cut Background Swaps (Synthetic Compositing)**:
   - Extract transparent PNG cut-outs of the tray (using the clean assets generated by `src/assets_prep.py` with pen marks removed).
   - Composite the tray onto authentic retail backgrounds:
     - Stainless steel refrigerated grocery cooler shelving
     - Red wire shopping cart grid floor
     - Wood grain display stands and butcher block counters
     - White commercial checkout conveyor belt
   - Eliminates model bias toward plain studio backdrops.

3. **Lighting & Specular Glare Variations**:
   - Apply gamma, color temperature, and specular highlight augmentations:
     - **Cold Store Light (4000K–5000K)**: Simulates commercial supermarket ceiling fluorescent strip tubes.
     - **Directional Warm Accent Light (3000K)**: Simulates butcher case spotlighting.
     - **Taut MAP Film Highlight Streaks**: Adds subtle specular streaks across the clear barrier film surface to teach the model natural plastic film reflections without distorting the underlying meat cut or fiber rim.

4. **Geometry & Strict Flip Rules**:
   - **Symmetric Views ONLY (Permitted)**: Horizontal flips ($x$-axis inversion) are **strictly limited** to unprinted, symmetric raw trays without text or meat labels (e.g., bare tray underside, plain top-down cavity, or symmetric side fluting).
   - **Asymmetric Views (STRICTLY PROHIBITED FROM FLIPPING)**:
     - Any image displaying the Trader Joe's paper sleeve label (sleeve is positioned on the left side; mirroring places it erroneously on the right).
     - Any image showing the debossed `"CIRKLA"` or patent text (mirroring produces backwards lettering).
     - Any image with handedness (e.g. thumb grip orientation).

## 2. Exclusions & Data Hygiene

To avoid poisoning the model's geometric priors, the following artifacts must be strictly quarantined from the training set:

1. **Handwritten Pen Marks**:
   - Raw trays #1, #2, and #3 contain blue ballpoint pen ink marks (`"ITC"` markings on the rim flange).
   - **Rule**: Never feed uncleaned raw images into the dataset. Only use assets processed through the `assets_prep.py` inpainting pipeline where pen marks have been eliminated and replaced with natural beige fiber grain.
2. **Hands Covering Essential Geometry**:
   - Exclude any reference photo where fingers or thumbs completely obscure the corner pyramids or wrap over the sidewall fluting.
   - The LoRA must learn the full, unabridged physical geometry of the packaging; human grip occlusion is handled later during generation conditioning.
3. **Motion Blur & Compression Artifacts**:
   - Exclude any frame with camera shake or severe JPEG macro-blocking.
   - All training images must be standardized to square or aspect-bucketed resolutions (e.g., $1024 \times 1024$, $896 \times 1152$, $768 \times 1024$) using Lanczos resampling.

## 3. Captioning Schema & Trigger Word

Every training image is accompanied by an exact companion `.txt` file using the defined trigger token:

$$\text{Trigger Word: } \mathbf{cirkla\_tray5}$$

### 3.1 Captioning Structure

```
cirkla_tray5, [shot angle], [tray geometry details], [material & color], [packaging features], [lighting & environment]
```

### 3.2 Canonical Caption Examples

- **Front Packaging View**:
  ```text
  cirkla_tray5, front medium view, natural beige unbleached molded plant fiber meat tray, vertical paper label sleeve on left side with Trader Joe's organic beef typography, fresh raw marbled angus beef on right, tight clear MAP barrier film sealed flush to flat perimeter rim flange, cold retail supermarket lighting
  ```
- **Side Profile (Fluting Focus)**:
  ```text
  cirkla_tray5, side profile 45-degree angle, showing 1.7-inch wall depth with distinct vertical scalloped ribs and structural fluting, natural beige molded fiber texture, uniform flat sealing flange, cold refrigerated grocery shelf background
  ```
- **Corner Detail (Pyramid Focus)**:
  ```text
  cirkla_tray5, macro closeup, molded corner reinforcement pyramid, structural corner feature for stacking rigidity, textured natural plant pulp fiber, taut clear packaging film edge, grocery aisle lighting
  ```
- **Underside Base View**:
  ```text
  cirkla_tray5, underside bottom view, recessed rectangular base panel with smooth rounded corner transitions, embossed CIRKLA mark, natural unbleached beige pulp texture, neutral studio background
  ```

## 4. Rigorous Evaluation Protocol (With vs. Without LoRA)

The LoRA's performance will be quantitatively benchmarked against the baseline reference-only model using CirklaGen's automated QA auditing suite (`src/qa.py` and `scripts/run_examples.py`).

### 4.1 A/B Benchmark Matrix

| Evaluation Dimension | Baseline (Reference-Only Prompting) | Fine-Tuned (With `cirkla_tray5` LoRA) | Target Delta |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| **DINOv2 Cosine Similarity** | $\approx 0.25 - 0.35$ | $\ge 0.55$ | $+60\%$ fidelity |
| **Checklist Pass Rate** | $70\% - 85\%$ | $\ge 95\%$ | $+15\%$ consistency |
| **Rib Fluting Retention** | Frequent hallucination into smooth plastic | 100% preservation of scalloped ribs | Qualitative Pass |
| **Corner Pyramids** | Often smoothed out or lost in perspective | Intact in $90\%+$ of generated angles | Qualitative Pass |
| **Exterior Color Drift** | Tendency to drift to white styrofoam / gray | Strictly locked to unbleached beige | Zero drift |

### 4.2 Automated Testing Pipeline

1. **Generate 20 Test Images (4 across each of the 5 shot types)**:
   - Run A: Baseline generator without LoRA (`generate(plate, refs, prompt, n=4)`).
   - Run B: Fine-tuned generator with LoRA weights loaded at strength `0.80`.
2. **Execute Automated QA Evaluator**:
   ```powershell
   python scripts/run_examples.py --out-dir outputs/examples/lora_bakeoff
   python scripts/build_report.py --csv outputs/examples/lora_bakeoff/results.csv
   ```
3. **Decision Criteria**:
   - The LoRA is deemed **production-ready** only if:
     1. Overall QA checklist pass rate exceeds **95%**.
     2. DINOv2 cosine similarity against ground-truth tray references increases by at least **0.20** over the baseline.
     3. Zero instances of blue pen marks or color drift to white/gray are observed across all 20 test candidates.
